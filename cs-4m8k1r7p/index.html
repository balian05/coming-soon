About Collettivo Studios

Creative & Brand Identity Institute — London

Collettivo Studios is a London-based creative and brand identity institute — not an agency in the conventional sense, but a long-horizon platform for original creative work. Brand strategy and visual design are handled in-house; photography and video production are delivered through the studio's collaborator network.

"We are The Collettivo Studios. Here, the future is born through sound and vision, and concept is the driving force behind everything. Without deep meaning and a clear idea, there is nothing. As a team of innovators we bring boldness and energy to every project, creating works that are a point of intersection between the old world and the new world. Our mission is to expand horizons and leave a vivid, memorable mark in the creative world, where meaning always precedes aesthetics."

Strategy first, design second. Every project at Collettivo Studios begins with brand strategy, not visual exploration — positioning, tone of voice, and a clear point of view before a single color or typeface is chosen. Content strategy and marketing strategy are built on that same foundation, so that what a brand says and where it says it are as deliberate as how it looks. Design is the last step, not the first — the visual layer that expresses a strategy already in place, never a substitute for one.

Rather than operating purely on client commissions, Collettivo Studios builds and holds a family of self-owned creative ventures guided by that same principle: Sound Molecules, a B2B audio-identity platform for hospitality and boutique retail venues, and Fashion Repubblica, a runway production house built around the proprietary WalkBeat© methodology for tempo-synchronized show scoring.

Collettivo Studios describes itself as an institute because its purpose extends beyond delivering client work: it is a sustained effort to shape how brands think about sound, image, and identity together, one project at a time.
